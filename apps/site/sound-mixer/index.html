sound-mixer

One audio context with real buses: levels above 1, ducking that reaches every voice, and a limiter at the end. A typed registry of sounds with variants and voice caps, streamed music, slow motion, and sounds placed in a scene. No dependencies.

bun add @zkmake/sound-mixer

The panel on the right is sound-desk

          
            A separate package, @zkmake/sound-desk: a dev panel for this mixer. It has
            no demo page of its own; it needs a live mix to show, so it's mounted here.
          
        

        
    
          
  • 
            mix: a meter, level, mute and solo for each bus. Hold to duck and watch music and
            ambience dip.
          
    • 
          
  • 
            voices: what's playing, by sound, with a 30-second trend that flags a leak.
          
    • 
          
  • 
            log: drops and steals. Press Burst ×16 and the coins past the cap of 6 show up as
            steals.
          
    • 
          
  • memory: each sample's decoded size, flagging anything that should stream.
    • 
          
  • scenes: fire the meadow's emitters, set its bed, play and skip music.
    • 
        

        
import { mountSoundDesk } from "@zkmake/sound-desk";
